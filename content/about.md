A blog was originally a weblog: a log of what someone was reading, doing, thinking. This is that.

I'm Chinmay Sharma, studying Computer Science and Computational Natural Sciences at IIIT Hyderabad. This blog is inspired by [bollu.github.io](https://bollu.github.io/) — I use it to log whatever I'm learning in math or development.

The posts may not be coherent to you. They're comprehensible to me. If you want to understand one better, feed it to an LLM and ask it to expand.

Things I have been or am interested in learning about:

- Statistical mechanics
- Category theory
- Differential geometry
- Nonlinear dynamics
- Network science
- Mathematical biology
- Machine learning, interpretability and alignment
- Modelling physical and biological phenomena with all of the above
- Eastern metaphysics — Yoga, Advaita Vedanta, Kashmiri Shaivism, Buddhism
- Psychoanalysis and philosophies with strange enough views of the world (this blog is named after a concept from D&G)
